South America

Lobster Claws

The Hanging Lobster Claw (Heliconia rostrata) or False Bird of Paradise, is a herbaceous perennial plant native to El Salvador, Peru, Bolivia, Colombia, Venezuela, Costa Rica, and Ecuador, and naturalized in Puerto Rico. It is found in tropical rainforests, as it thrives in warm and humid environments.

Along with the cantuta flower, Heliconia rostrata, known as patujú, is the national flower of Bolivia.

Peruvian Lily

Peruvian Lily (Alstroemeria), or the Lily of the Incas is a vibrant perennial flowering plant native to South America that produces long-lasting, trumpet-shaped blooms.

Cat's Claw

Cat's Claw Dolichandra unguis-cati, is a fast-growing, high-climbing woody vine featuring bright yellow, trumpet-shaped flowers and claw-like tendrils.

Invasiveness

Highly Aggresive; although it is native to many parts of South America, it is classified as a severe invasive weed in places like Florida, Hawaii, Australia, and South Africa because it smothers native trees and destroys forest ecosystems.

Detailed management information can be found through the University of Florida IFAS Extension
