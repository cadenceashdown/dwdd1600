Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citinzenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, ethical, and safe use of technology and the internet. It encompasses how you interact with others, evaluate information, protect your privacy, and navigate online spaces respectfully.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law is the legal framework and set of rules governing electronic behavior, online interactions, and technology use.

What is AUP?

An AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a formal document that defines the rules, regulations, and guidelines for using a organization's network, internet connection, and digital hardware.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of taking someone else's words, ideas, images, or creative work and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.

Within digital citizenship, avoiding plagiarism is a key part of digital ethics, intellectual property rights, and managing your digital footprint.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty refers to any action or attempted action that gives a student an unfair advantage in an academic setting or misrepresents their actual learning, knowledge, or effort.

It violates the ethical principles of educational institutions and undermines the value of degrees and academic evaluation.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

The rules for AI-generated content depend on the context in which it is used-whether in academia, legal/copyright frameworks, or platform terms of service. Across all domains, the overarching principle is transparency, accountability, and ethical use.
